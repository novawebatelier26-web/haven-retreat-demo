GOOD TO KNOW

Before You Arrive

A professional lodge website should answer important questions before guests need to ask.

Pool Safety

Publish verified pool depth, fencing information, supervision rules and access arrangements.

Day Visitors & Events

State whether day visitors, birthdays and small private gatherings are permitted.

Pets & Children

Clearly explain pet-friendly accommodation, child policies and pool-area restrictions.

Booking & Cancellation

Provide confirmed deposits, cancellation terms, seasonal prices and check-in information.
